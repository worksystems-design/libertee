# Futures Cone — Example Session (Brief Mode)

## Command

```
/libertee:futures-cone "How will knowledge work look in 5 years?" --brief
```

## What Happens

✋ **Facilitator** sets the question:

> *Working on a 5-year horizon. Four zones: probable, plausible, possible, preposterous. We're not predicting — we're mapping the space and looking for decisions that survive across all of it.*

### 🔭 Scenarios

**Probable — "AI-assisted but team-shaped"**
Most knowledge workers spend most of their time in teams of 3-12, AI agents handle drafts, summaries, and routine analysis, the human-team rhythm stays roughly recognizable.
*Driver:* Steady AI capability gains in defined tasks, slow institutional change.

**Plausible — "Solo with agent-fleets"**
Many knowledge workers operate as single principals managing 5-50 specialized AI agents, "team" becomes a coordination function across agent fleets rather than a colleague-cluster.
*Driver:* Agent reliability crosses a threshold; org structure follows tooling.

**Possible — "Outcome-pricing dissolves the role"**
Companies stop paying for knowledge-worker hours and start paying per outcome; whoever delivers (human, agent, or hybrid) gets paid; "knowledge worker" as a job category fades.
*Driver:* Outcome-measurement becomes cheap and universal.

**Preposterous — "Cognitive commons"**
Most knowledge work is published into shared substrates (open commons of cases, models, decisions) and rewarded through reputation in the commons; companies become curators rather than employers.
*Driver:* Trust infrastructure makes credentialing portable across organizations.

### 📡 Indicators

- Probable: ratio of "AI-augmented productivity" claims in earnings calls (already present, growing).
- Plausible: rise of "fractional everything" patterns and one-person-multi-agent setups (early signs in solo founder space).
- Possible: outcome-based contracting in professional services (visible in legal-tech and dev-contractor space, not yet visible in enterprise).
- Preposterous: portable reputation systems linked to verifiable contributions (not yet visible at scale; watch ENS-style identity layers).

### 🛡️ Robustness

**Robust across all four zones:** *Build skills that survive any of these futures* — judgment, taste, institutional context, decision quality. These are valuable whether your unit of work is a team-task, an agent-fleet outcome, or a commons contribution.

**Brittle in plausible / possible / preposterous:** *Career strategies that depend on stable team-affiliation as the unit of identity.* The "I work at company X" framing assumes the probable zone; in the other three, identity attaches to outcomes or commons-contributions instead.

### 🎯 The Robust Portfolio

- Invest in judgment and taste — useful in every zone.
- Detach professional identity from team-affiliation — fragile in 3 of 4 zones.
- Watch outcome-pricing in legal/dev-contractor space as the leading indicator for the "possible" zone moving toward "plausible."

**💡 The Hidden Future:** The cone itself assumes "knowledge work" remains a recognized category. The most disruptive scenario is one where the *category* dissolves — and the design that survives that is one built around capabilities, not occupations.
