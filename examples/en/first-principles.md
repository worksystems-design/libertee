# First Principles — Example Session (Brief Mode)

## Command

```
/libertee:first-principles "We need a weekly status meeting" --brief
```

## What Happens

✋ **Facilitator** sets the claim:

> *Claim on the table: "We need a weekly status meeting." We'll ask "why?" until we hit either bedrock or convention, then sort, then rebuild.*

### 🤔 Decomposition

**Level 1 — Why must we have a weekly status meeting?**
- Answer: So leadership knows what each team is working on.
- Hidden assumptions: leadership needs to know weekly · "knowing" requires synchronous reporting · the meeting is the right reporting venue.

**Level 2 — Why does leadership need to know weekly?**
- Answer: To intervene early when projects drift.
- Hidden assumptions: drift is detectable from a status update · weekly is the right cadence for intervention · intervention is leadership's job, not the team's.

**Terminus:** Convention — "weekly status" is inherited from manufacturing-era management cadences, not from a derived necessity.

### ⚖️ Necessity vs. Convention

| # | Assumption | Category |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leadership needs visibility into work | Logical (if accountability exists) |
| 2 | Visibility requires synchronous reporting | Convention |
| 3 | Meeting is the right venue | Convention |
| 4 | Weekly is the right cadence | Untested |
| 5 | Drift is detectable from status updates | Untested |
| 6 | Intervention is leadership's job | Convention |

**Contradictions found:** None.

### 🔨 Reconstruction

**Alternative 1 — Async-first dashboard.** A live dashboard updated by teams as work changes; leadership reads when they want, intervenes when an indicator turns red. Drops: meeting venue, weekly cadence. Keeps: visibility into work.

**Alternative 2 — Trigger-based escalation.** No standing meeting. Teams flag drift themselves when defined thresholds are crossed; leadership joins those triggered conversations. Drops: meeting, weekly cadence, leadership-as-monitor. Keeps: accountability for drift detection (now distributed to teams).

**💡 The Hidden Move:** "Visibility" is not the same as "synchronous report." Treating them as equivalent is the convention everyone treats as physics — and it's the move that opens up two completely different designs.
