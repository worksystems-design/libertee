# TRIZ — Example Session (Brief Mode)

## Command

```
/libertee:triz "Our ability to make decisions that stick across the organization" --brief
```

## What Happens

😈 **Facilitator** sets the scene:

> *Let's flip this around. Your mission: guarantee that no decision ever sticks in this organization. How would you sabotage it?*

### 💣 Sabotage Strategies

1. **The Committee Labyrinth** — Ensure every decision requires sign-off from at least three committees that meet on different continents in rotating time zones, so by the time approval lands, the context has evaporated.

2. **The Infinite Reopen** — Cultivate a cultural norm where any team can reopen any decision by simply saying "but did we really consider...?" in a Slack thread, with no burden of proof required.

3. **The Accountability Gap** — Make sure the person who made the decision is never the one who has to implement it, so accountability and ownership live in permanently separate zip codes.

4. **The Documentation Flood** — Flood every decision with so much documentation, rationale, and stakeholder input that no one can locate the actual decision itself, let alone remember what was decided.

5. **The Pivot Cult** — Celebrate pivoting as a virtue and consistency as rigidity, so reversing course is always framed as courageous leadership while sticking to a decision is evidence of closed-mindedness.

### 🪞 Reality Check

| Strategy | Verdict |
|----------|---------|
| 1. The Committee Labyrinth | **Drifting toward.** Architectural review boards and RFC processes are multiplying. |
| 2. The Infinite Reopen | **Already doing.** Standard behavior in retros and Slack threads; whoever reopens bears zero cost. |
| 3. The Accountability Gap | **Already doing.** PMs decide, engineers build; leadership sets direction, teams absorb consequences. |
| 4. The Documentation Flood | **Drifting toward.** Confluence pages exist; finding the operative sentence requires archaeology. |
| 5. The Pivot Cult | **Drifting toward.** "Agile responsiveness" framing makes reversal feel virtuous by default. |

### 😈 Facilitator — Stop Doing It

**🚨 Already Sabotaging Ourselves:**
- **Infinite Reopen** — anyone can say "but did we consider..." and the decision unravels. Stop: require the reopener to state what new information changed, or the decision stands.
- **Accountability Gap** — the person who decides never implements; the person who implements never decided. Stop: no decision is final until the implementer co-signs it.

**⚠️ Drifting Toward Sabotage:**
- **Committee Labyrinth** — review boards multiplying. Kill one approval layer this month as an experiment.
- **Documentation Flood** — decisions buried in Confluence. Every decision document gets a one-line "THE DECISION:" header at the top.
- **Pivot Cult** — reframe: changing course requires the same rigor as the original decision, not less.

**✅ First Actions:**
- Introduce a "burden of proof" rule for reopening decisions: new information required, or it stands
- Add a "THE DECISION" one-liner to the top of every decision doc
- Pair every strategic decision with the name of the person who implements it

**💡 The Pattern Behind the Pattern:**
The organization treats decisions as discussions that never formally close — there is no moment where a decision becomes *binding*, so every decision is perpetually provisional.
